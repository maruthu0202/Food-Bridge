About FoodBridge

FoodBridge is a platform dedicated to eliminating food waste by connecting surplus food from restaurants, hotels, bakeries, and individuals directly to NGOs and shelters that serve communities in need.

Our Mission

To create a zero-food-waste ecosystem where every kilogram of edible food finds its way to someone who truly needs it — reducing hunger one donation at a time.

Our Vision

A world where technology bridges the gap between surplus and scarcity — where no nutritious food is discarded while people go hungry.
